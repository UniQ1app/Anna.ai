# ANNA — Qubic AI (App & AGI)

ANNA is a Qubic-dedicated AI system designed to **explain Qubic accurately, safely, and at scale**.

ANNA is split into two clearly separated layers:

- **ANNA (App)** — the user-facing application  
- **ANNA (AGI)** — the Qubic-native intelligence behind the app  

This separation is intentional and non-negotiable.

ANNA does not execute actions, does not govern, and does not interact with the Qubic protocol.  
ANNA explains. Humans decide.

---

## What is ANNA (AGI)?
<p align="center">
  <img src="/2470010F-A9F3-47AB-A14D-9EF5F2DE8A01.png" alt="ANNA App Interfaces" width="900">
</p>

**ANNA (AGI)** is the intelligence layer responsible for:

- reasoning over verified Qubic knowledge  
- maintaining internal consistency  
- preventing hallucinations  
- evolving only through controlled, human-reviewed training  

ANNA (AGI):
- has no user interface  
- does not interact with users directly  
- cannot act or execute anything  
- is accessed only through **ANNA (App)**  

If information is not validated, ANNA (AGI) explicitly responds:
> “This information is not validated yet.”

---

## What is ANNA (App)?

**ANNA (App)** is a ChatGPT-like application (mobile & web).

It allows users to:
- ask questions in natural language  
- receive structured, verified answers  
- explore Qubic concepts safely  

ANNA (App) does not think by itself.  
It only mediates access to **ANNA (AGI)**.

---

## ANNA (App) — Interfaces

### 1. Main Chat Interface
- Chat-based UI
- Natural language questions
- Answers generated by ANNA (AGI)
- Clear refusal when information is uncertain

### 2. Knowledge Source Panel
- Displays sources used by ANNA (AGI)
- Links to validated documentation and repositories
- Full transparency

### 3. Trust, Gate & Burn Panel
- Displays user level (Public / Trusted / Gate)
- Shows Trust Score or Gate Score
- Shows QUBIC burn cost per interaction
- Shows activity history

---

## User Levels & Access

### Level 1 — Public Users
- Read-only access
- Can browse explanations
- No interaction with ANNA (AGI)

---

### Level 2 — Trusted Users

**How to become Trusted**
- Complete basic Qubic learning paths  
- Demonstrate clean, non-spam behavior  
- Connect a Qubic wallet  
- Accept burn-based interaction rules  

**What Trusted users can do**
- Ask questions via ANNA (App)
- Receive verified answers from ANNA (AGI)

**What they cannot do**
- Train or modify ANNA (AGI)
- Correct knowledge

---

### Level 3 — Gate Users (Qubic Scholars)

Gate Users are highly qualified experts.

**How to pass Gate**
- Complete advanced Qubic learning paths  
- Demonstrate deep technical understanding  
- Maintain long-term accuracy and consistency  
- Be reviewed and validated by existing Gate Users  
- No payment, no shortcut, no purchase possible  

**What Gate users can do**
- Review and correct ANNA (AGI) answers  
- Submit new verified knowledge  
- Participate in controlled training of ANNA (AGI)  

Only Gate Users can train ANNA (AGI).

---

## Trust Score System (Points-Based)

Trusted users receive a **Trust Score**, reflecting real engagement.

Trust Score increases with:
- wallet activity and seniority  
- learning progression  
- clean and relevant interactions  

Trust Score directly affects:
- interaction limits  
- access level  
- QUBIC burn cost  

Higher Trust Score → lower burn cost.

---

## Gate Score System

Gate Users have a **Gate Score**, measuring quality, not quantity.

Gate Score increases with:
- accurate corrections of ANNA (AGI)  
- verified training submissions  
- high-quality Qubic explanations (e.g. on X)  
- reviewing other Gate submissions  

Errors or abuse reduce the score.

Higher Gate Score → more training rights and lower (or zero) burn.

---

## QUBIC Burn Model

Interacting with ANNA (App) requires **burning QUBIC**.

Burning is intentional and permanent.

**Why burn QUBIC?**
- prevent spam and abuse  
- align AI usage with real economic cost  
- reduce total supply  
- reward long-term, serious users  

Burn logic:
- everyone burns QUBIC to interact  
- higher Trust/Gate score = lower burn  
- abuse = higher burn or access removal  

Burn is not a fee. Burned QUBIC is destroyed.

## Training ANNA (AGI) — Current Phase

### Training via X (Twitter)

1. Gate Users publish high-quality Qubic explanations on X  
2. Content is public, technical, and verifiable  
3. Submissions are reviewed  
4. Approved knowledge is ingested into ANNA (AGI)  

This ensures transparency, accountability, and high signal quality.

---

## Training Rules (Strict)

- No open training  
- No anonymous contributions  
- No self-learning loops  
- No speculative data  

All updates are:
- reviewed  
- versioned  
- reversible  
- traceable  

---

## Hard Limits & Safety

ANNA (App) and ANNA (AGI) will never:
- execute transactions  
- access wallets  
- interact with Qubic consensus  
- provide financial advice  
- speculate on prices or protocol changes
