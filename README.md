# ANNA — Qubic AI Interface

ANNA is an AI assistant dedicated to the **Qubic ecosystem**.

It works like ChatGPT in terms of interaction,
but it is fundamentally different in purpose and design.

ANNA is built to **explain Qubic accurately**,  
not to speculate, not to execute, and not to hallucinate.

---

## What is ANNA?

ANNA is a **Qubic-specific AI interface** designed to:

- explain Qubic concepts clearly
- answer technical and non-technical questions
- centralize Qubic knowledge
- prevent misinformation as the ecosystem grows

ANNA is an **information layer**, not an action layer.

---

## How is ANNA different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT:
- learns from the open internet
- can hallucinate
- has no domain restrictions

ANNA:
- answers **only from verified Qubic sources**
- is **source-bound and versioned**
- refuses to answer if information is not validated
- prioritizes correctness over fluency

---

## How ANNA Works (Simple)

ANNA is built around **three core rules**:

1. **Verified Sources Only**  
   Every answer comes from approved documentation, repositories, or validated material.

2. **Trust-Gated Interaction**  
   Not everyone has the same permissions.

3. **No Execution**  
   ANNA cannot perform actions, transactions, or protocol interactions.

---

## User Levels & Permissions

### 1️⃣ Public Users
- Can read documentation
- No AI interaction

---

### 2️⃣ Trusted Users
- Can ask questions to ANNA
- Receive answers based only on verified sources
- Cannot modify or train ANNA

This is similar to a **safe ChatGPT for Qubic**.

---

### 3️⃣ Gate Users (Qubic Scholars)

Gate Users are highly qualified community members who passed advanced validation stages.

They can:
- correct ANNA’s answers
- add or refine knowledge
- help train ANNA in a controlled way

Only Gate Users can **teach ANNA**.

---

## Training Rules (Very Important)

- ❌ No open training
- ❌ No anonymous edits
- ❌ No self-learning loops

Training is:
- reviewed
- versioned
- reversible
- traceable to contributors

This prevents hallucination and knowledge drift.

---

## What ANNA Does NOT Do

ANNA explicitly does NOT:
- execute transactions
- access wallets
- interact with consensus
- give financial advice
- speculate about future protocol changes

ANNA explains.
Humans decide.

---

## Relationship with Qubic

ANNA:
- does not modify the Qubic protocol
- does not influence computors
- does not introduce governance
- does not replace official documentation

It **augments understanding**, not authority.

---

## Why ANNA Matters

As Qubic grows:
- documentation becomes harder to navigate
- support does not scale
- misinformation becomes dangerous

ANNA allows:
- scalable education
- consistent answers
- protected knowledge integrity
